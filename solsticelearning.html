About us

Solstice Learning is a small online tutoring practice in Richmond, British Columbia. We work with Grade 9 to 12 students on the BC provincial curriculum — not a generic international syllabus, and not worksheet packets bought off a shelf.

Teaching here is done by tutors with classroom experience in BC secondary schools and subject training at the university level. We keep the roster small on purpose: every student holds a fixed weekly slot with the same tutor for the whole term.

Our teaching includes a TEFL-certified tutor with classroom experience taking ELL students through STEM subjects. Science and mathematics carry vocabulary of their own — lab reports, word problems, the exact wording of an exam question — and that is often where a capable student who is still building English loses marks that have nothing to do with the science. For families arriving into the Canadian high school system partway through, this is usually the support that decides how smooth the transition is.

Built around the BC curriculum

We teach named BC courses to the provincial learning standards. Sessions track your child's actual class — the unit they are on, the assignment due Friday, the exam in three weeks.

Small by design

We cap how many students we take each term. That is what makes a fixed weekly time, the same tutor every week, and a real answer within a school day possible.

Progress you can read

After every session you get a short written note on what was covered and what still needs work. No guessing whether the money is doing anything.

Questions parents ask

Are sessions online or in person?

Online, one-on-one, over video. That is how we keep a fixed weekly time that works around sports, part-time jobs, and family schedules.

Can we speak Chinese with you?

Yes. Consults, progress reports, and billing questions can all be handled in Mandarin or English. Sessions themselves are taught in English, since that is the language of the provincial exams.

Do you teach the sciences and maths in depth?

Those are our main focus. Biology, Chemistry, Pre-Calculus and Calculus make up most of what we teach, and sessions run concept-first before moving to timed past-paper practice.

What does ELL support involve?

Academic English for students still building fluency — the reading, note-taking, and written work their other courses assume. It runs alongside the subject timetable rather than replacing it.

What if we need to miss a week?

Give us 24 hours' notice and the session is rescheduled at no cost. Semester plans include two makeup sessions on top of that.

Do you cover the course my child is actually in?

Check the course list above. If it is not there, we will tell you rather than take the booking — and where we can, we will point you to someone who does cover it.
